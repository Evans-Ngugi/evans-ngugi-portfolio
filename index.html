About Me

I am a water engineer with 9+ years of experience supporting water and sanitation infrastructure for four utilities and a national water-sector fund in Kenya. I review engineering drawings and as-builts against field conditions, keep infrastructure records accurate and current, and prepare progress and regulatory reports for management and WASREB. I'm passionate about using geospatial data and sound engineering to help utilities deliver reliable, safe water and reduce water losses.
